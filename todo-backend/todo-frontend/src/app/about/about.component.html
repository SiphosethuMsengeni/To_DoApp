About this app

Simple task tracking with a polished workflow

This Todo App helps you capture tasks, set priorities, add due dates, search quickly, and keep track of progress in one place. It is designed to stay lightweight on the backend while still feeling clean and job-ready on the frontend.

Track work

Create tasks, mark them complete, and keep everything organized without friction.

Stay focused

Use filters and search to surface active tasks, completed work, or anything that needs attention.

Show progress

Progress tracking and overdue highlighting make it easier to see what is done and what still needs work.
